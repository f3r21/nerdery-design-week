Vello: post a request

A requester posts a need to verified neighbors nearby, then books one who replies.

Source: vello/05-prototype-screens-jsx.md (cited L<n>) Interviews: Vello_Interview_Transcripts/ (cited P01–P06) Brief: vello-product.vercel.app, read 17 Sept

Weakest part. The prototype has no network layer, so every endpoint, API status, and server-error state is marked [ASSUMPTION]. v2 adds a second weak spot: the unhappy paths now assume a provider can accept or decline a booking. That's sourced from a status value (BK_STATUS.pending, L425) and a seeded booking, not from any screen. If bookings are meant to be instant, B11–B14 collapse.

What v2 adds. Three stress tests — a provider who never responds, every place a requester can cancel, and what an admin sees when verification fails — folded into the contract as rows, endpoints and two new figures, plus the revision log of what three audit passes found.

Stress test

Three questions, each answered the same way: what the source does today, what the revised contract does, and what stays unresolved.

Q1 · What if the provider never responds? two silent points, not one

The prototype has no timer anywhere. A request with no replies says “Neighbors usually answer within a couple of hours. We'll let you know the moment someone does” (L776–781) and then waits forever; the Home card sits on “Waiting for replies” (L547). A newly posted request can't receive replies at all (L2153). The second silent point is already named in the data: BK_STATUS.pending is “Waiting on reply” (L425) and booking b2 ships in that state (L411) — but Confirm jumps straight to confirmed (L1129) and Manage opens every booking as confirmed (L2229), so the state has no screen.

Revised: a quiet request gets nudged, not expired — your call this round. At T1 the requester sees “Still quiet on your block” with three ways out (widen the budget, change when, browse neighbors) and nearby providers are re-notified (R7, R8, R10). A booking starts pending; the neighbor accepts (B12), declines (B13, and the other replies stay open), or stays silent (B14, nudge · cancel · book someone else). A neighbor cancelling late is its own state (B20) because P01 says that is what ends the relationship (P01 L45).

Unresolved: T1 and T2, and whether a pending booking should ever expire on its own.

Unresolved: which policy is real, and whether closing a request cancels a booking that's still pending on it (R15).

Q3 · What does the admin see if verification fails? the queue is real, the verification outcome isn't

An ID check is a queue item like any other: q1 “ID photo doesn't match selfie”, High, 31h old, with evidence and account history (L345–349). The admin can Approve (L1800), Escalate — which toasts “escalated to legal” (L1804) — or Reject, which forces a reason (L1789) and then removes the item with a toast (L1703–1717). Four things are missing, and each one is a state: none of the five reasons fits a failed ID check (L395), there is nothing between yes and no (P06: “I didn't have anything in between”), the decision leaves no record, and the applicant has no screen at all (P04 waited two weeks and chased twice, L61).

Unresolved: whether a rejected applicant can reapply, who handles payment disputes if the admin is a volunteer, and what happens to replies and bookings when a verified provider is later rejected (P8).

Defaults I chose that you never set

  • REST + JSON, no version prefix; replies embedded in GET /requests/{id}.
  • Close = PATCH status, not DELETE; 422 validation, 409 conflict, 402 payment.
  • Idempotency-Key on both POSTs; errors always keep the user's input.
  • Your “nudge, never expire” call applied to pending bookings too — the requester cancels instead.
  • A nudge is rate-limited (429) rather than unlimited.
  • Verification decisions are one endpoint with a decision enum, not four routes.
  • Scope still ends at booking confirmed; payment, rating and messages stay off-page.
