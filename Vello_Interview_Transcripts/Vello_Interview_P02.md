# Vello discovery interviews — P02

| **Participant** | P02 |

| **Date** | 2026-06-12 |
| **Length** | 34 min |
| **Interviewer** | R1 |
| **Notes** | Recruited via a flyer at the health centre. Baby present for part of the session. |

---

**R1:** Tell me about hiring someone locally since you moved here.

**P02:** Sure. So the honest answer is it's been harder than I thought. We moved in October, the baby came in December, and we basically have no one. Where we lived before I had a guy for everything. I had a guy for the boiler, I had a guy for the car. Twelve years of building that up and then you move six miles and you're back to zero.

**R1:** What have you needed?

**P02:** Boiler, immediately, in November, which was a nightmare. A cleaner, we've got someone now. And we're starting to look for childcare which is the one that scares me.

**R1:** Say more about that.

**P02:** Look, I'll be blunt with you. If somebody is going to be alone with my son, I want to see a check. I want to see a DBS, I want to see references I can call, I want to see ID. I don't understand how any of these apps operate without that. It's the wild west. You've got these platforms where anyone can sign up with an email address and a photo and then they're in your house with your child. That's insane to me. That should be illegal.

**R1:** Has that stopped you from using them?

**P02:** It's stopped me trusting them. I look, I browse, and then I close it. My wife says I'm paranoid. I'm not paranoid, I'm just, you read things.

**R1:** You mentioned you found a cleaner. How did that go?

**P02:** That was through the woman upstairs, actually. Denise. She's been here forever, she knows everyone. She said try Marta, Marta does her place on Thursdays, so Marta does ours on Thursdays now too. Works out well because she's already in the building.

**R1:** What did you check before Marta started?

**P02:** Nothing, really. [pause] Well, no, I mean, Denise vouched for her. That's the check.

**R1:** So that's different from the app situation.

**P02:** It's completely different. Denise has known her for four years. She's not going to put her name on someone who's going to rob me. And Marta's got a key now, so, yeah. [laughs] Alright, I hear it. I hear what you're doing. But it is different. It's a person taking responsibility, that's not the same as a company saying trust us.

**R1:** Would a check from the platform have made you feel better about Marta?

**P02:** It wouldn't have changed anything. I already had Denise. I suppose the checks are for when you don't have a Denise.

**R1:** Which was your situation in November, with the boiler.

**P02:** Exactly. In November I had no one and I called the first number I found on Google and paid four hundred quid and I still don't know if that was a fair price. That's the actual problem. Not safety. I was never in danger from the boiler man. I just had no way to know if I was being had.

**R1:** How long do you think it takes to build up a Denise?

**P02:** Years? I mean, Denise took eight months and I got lucky because she's the sort of person who knocks on your door with a cake. If she wasn't there I'd have nobody. There's forty flats on this street and I could name three people.

**R1:** Is there anything that would speed that up?

**P02:** I don't know. The neighbourhood association do a thing, a summer fair, and we went, and it was nice, but you don't come away from that with a plumber. Everyone tells you word of mouth, word of mouth, and it's like, yes, but I need a mouth. I need someone to be talking to me first.

**R1:** If an app showed you that a neighbour had used someone, would that help?

**P02:** Which neighbour, though? If it says a neighbour used them, that's nothing, that's just a stranger who lives nearby. It has to be Denise. It has to be a name I recognise. And I've only got three names, so, that's a very small list. Maybe that gets better in a few years.

**R1:** What about ratings and reviews?

**P02:** I read them but I don't believe them. Everyone's five stars. The only reviews I trust are the bad ones and there are never any bad ones, which tells you something. Either everyone's brilliant or nobody leaves the bad ones.

**R1:** Last question. What's the one thing you'd want from something like this?

**P02:** Proper vetting. Real checks, real ID, real references, and don't let people on without it. If you did that I'd use it tomorrow, honestly, and I'd pay more for it. That's the gap in the market as far as I'm concerned.
