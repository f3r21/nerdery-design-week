# Vello discovery interviews — P04

| | |
|---|---|
| **Participant** | P04 |
| **Role** | Provider (house cleaning, some ironing) |
| **Context** | 44, self-employed six years, works across Kestrel Park and two adjacent areas, lives fifteen minutes away by bus |
| **Date** | 2026-06-16 |
| **Length** | 45 min |
| **Interviewer** | R1 |
| **Notes** | Interviewed between jobs, in a cafe. Some background noise. |

---

**R1:** Walk me through a normal week.

**P04:** Monday is two houses in the morning, one in the afternoon. Tuesday, three. Wednesday I keep for the office job, I do a small office. Thursday and Friday are houses again. Saturday sometimes if someone's desperate, but I try not to.

**R1:** How many clients in total?

**P04:** Regular, about fourteen. Then there's people who call twice a year for the big clean, before Christmas or if they're selling.

**R1:** How did you get to fourteen?

**P04:** One at a time. My first was Mrs. Aldridge, that's nine years ago now, and she told her sister, and the sister told someone at her book group. Almost all of them come from each other. I've never advertised. I did put a card in the newsagent once and got nothing.

**R1:** So recommendation is the whole channel.

**P04:** It's the whole thing. And it's slow. If I want two new clients this month, I can't make that happen. I just have to wait for someone to move away or have a baby.

**R1:** Are your clients all in one area?

**P04:** No, and this is a thing for me. Kestrel Park is maybe half of them. Then I've got four the other side of the main road, and three up towards the station. It's three different worlds, really. Nobody in Kestrel Park knows anybody up by the station.

**R1:** Does the spread cause problems?

**P04:** Time. Just time. I've got a gap Tuesdays because getting from one to the other is forty minutes with the bus, so I can't fill it. If they were all on the same street I'd earn more for the same hours. But you can't choose, you take who calls you.

**R1:** If a platform only let you work in one neighbourhood, how would that land?

**P04:** Then I'd lose half my income, so I wouldn't use it. [laughs] I understand why they'd do it, the local thing is nice, but I don't live in Kestrel Park either. I can't afford Kestrel Park. Most of the people cleaning those houses live somewhere else. So if it's neighbours only, where does that leave me?

**R1:** That's a good point. Do clients care that you live elsewhere?

**P04:** They've never asked. Not once, in nine years. They care that I come on the day I said and that I'm the same person every week. That's it. That's the whole job.

**R1:** Say more about being the same person.

**P04:** The agencies send whoever's free. Different girl every time, and you have to explain everything again, where the hoover is, don't use bleach on that. People hate it. When they come to me it's because they had that and they got sick of it. So the thing I'm selling isn't really cleaning, everyone can clean. It's that it's me, and I know your house, and I'll be there Thursday.

**R1:** Have you used any of the apps?

**P04:** I signed up for one, two years ago, when I lost two clients at once and I panicked. It was, honestly, it was demoralising. You're in a list with forty people and it's all price. Someone's charging twelve an hour and I can't, I won't work for that. I got two jobs off it and both of them were one-offs and one of them left a bad review because I wouldn't do the oven, which wasn't in the booking.

**R1:** Did you challenge the review?

**P04:** There was nothing to challenge it with. There's a button, I pressed it, nothing happened. That review is still there and it's the only one I've got.

**R1:** How long did signing up take?

**P04:** Ages. I had to send my ID and then wait, and I chased them twice, and it was about two weeks before I could actually appear. And in that two weeks I could have been working. If you're doing that to people who need money now, you'll lose them.

**R1:** What would make a platform worth being on?

**P04:** Fill my gaps. That's it. I don't need it to replace my clients, I've got clients. I need Tuesday afternoon filled, near the other Tuesday jobs, with someone who'll rebook. If it just sends me one-off strangers across town it's making my week worse, not better.

**R1:** And what about how you're paid?

**P04:** I'd want to know the number up front. Some of them take a percentage of every job forever, which, after two years with the same client, what exactly am I paying for? If it's a fee a month I can work that out and decide. A cut of everything feels like it never ends.

**R1:** Last thing. What builds trust with a new client, in your experience?

**P04:** First three visits. If I'm on time three times, we're fine after that, they stop watching. And they leave me the key at the fourth. You can always tell. The key is the moment.
